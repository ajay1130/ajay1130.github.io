01. About Me

Turning complex business needs into apps people love

I'm a Mobile Application Developer with 5 years of experience designing, building and shipping cross-platform and native apps using Flutter, Dart, Kotlin and Jetpack Compose — serving millions of end users on real-time, high-traffic platforms.

At Brainvire Infotech, I owned modules end-to-end across fintech, e-commerce, social, communication, field-operations and sports-booking domains — from requirement analysis and architecture through testing, debugging and production releases on the App Store and Google Play.

My sweet spot: real-time data (WebSockets, streams), clean, scalable architecture (Clean Architecture, BLoC, MVVM) and smooth, polished UX — working closely with backend, QA and product teams to ship quality software.

Beyond client work, I independently designed, built and published PitchIQ — a cricket pitch-report app on the Play Store — taking it from concept and data model to decision logic, UI and release.
